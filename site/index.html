About Pasado

Every PRC board exam, the Bar and the Civil Service Exam, counting down. Find your exam, star it, and share the countdown with your batchmates.

Where the dates come from. PRC's yearly Schedule of Examinations, read once a day. Reschedules and resolutions PRC adds to that page show up here as notices. Bar dates come from the Supreme Court's Bar Bulletins, and Civil Service Exam dates from the CSC's exam announcements; each links to its source.

Estimated dates. Until PRC publishes next year's schedule, those sittings are projected from this year's (same weekday, a year later) and marked Estimated. They're for planning only and never go into calendar feeds.

Times. Everything is Philippine time. Exam and results countdowns run to the start of the day; "apply by" runs to the end of the last filing day.
