Exam dates

From the Professional Regulation Commission's public Schedule of Examinations, read once or twice a day. Pasado reorganizes those dates (application window, exam days, target results date, testing centers and the resolutions PRC adds) into countdowns and calendar feeds. Dates marked “Estimated” are our own projection from the previous year, not PRC's.

Bar Examination dates come from the Supreme Court's Bar Bulletins, and Civil Service Examination dates from the Civil Service Commission's examination announcements. We enter those by hand, and each sitting links to the document it came from.

Pasado is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, PRC, the Supreme Court or the CSC. Always confirm dates and requirements on the official site before you apply.
